# About surface

- Scope: `/about`, implemented by `app/about/page.tsx`.
- Visitor mode: Read.
- Audience: Georgian customers who want a quick, credible understanding of what Home Mix offers.
- Job: Understand the catalog's purpose and continue to the products without reading a long company story.
- Primary action: Open the product catalog.
- Proof and content: One truthful explanation of the live catalog and one established room photograph.
- Constraints: Short copy; no fabricated history, team, scale, or commercial claims; Georgian-first; mobile-first; established warm storefront world.
- Chosen direction: A quiet split composition pairing the concise explanation with one generous living-room image.
- Memorable moment: The page feels complete in one viewport instead of stretching a small amount of truth into multiple sections.
- Unresolved decisions: Real company history and team information can be added only when supplied.
